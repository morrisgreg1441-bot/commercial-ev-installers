## 3. The 60-minute weekend plan

Reality: one weekend ≈ 8 focused hours after research overhead. Allocate as **45 min research + 15 min sending per outlet, 10 outlets max.** Do not exceed 10. Quality decays sharply after that.

### Hour-by-hour

**Hour 0 (Saturday AM, 60 min) — prep, do once:**
1. Open `/data/uk-ev-installer-landscape/`. Write down the 3 most striking specific stats (e.g. "NE England: 18 installers / 23k businesses"). These become subject lines.
2. Verify each Tier 1–2 outlet's current EV/fleet reporter via byline search on their site (last 30 days). If they haven't published on EV/fleet in 90 days, drop them.
3. Find emails via Hunter.io free tier (25/mo) or LinkedIn → company website → masthead.

**Hour 1 — Tier 1 trade press (4 sends, Template A):**
- Fleet News named reporter
- Fleet World editor
- Commercial Fleet EV reporter
- Air Quality News editor

**Hour 2 — Tier 2 sustainability (2 sends, Template A):**
- BusinessGreen
- edie.net

**Hour 3 — Tier 3 regional (2 sends, Template A, local-stat variant):**
- Pick the 2 worst-served regions only. Insider Media or BusinessLive for each.

**Hour 4 — Tier 4 associations (2 sends, Template C):**
- BVRLA
- AFP

**Hour 5 — Tier 6 community (organic, 30 min):**
- SpeakEV: find one installer-recommendation thread, reply usefully, link only if answering
- r/electricvehicles: same
- LinkedIn: one post (not a DM blast) showing the regional map image with the headline stat. Tag no one.

**Hour 6 — Tier 7 monitoring setup (15 min):**
- Set up #JournoRequest saved search on X/Bluesky
- Free ResponseSource alerts for "EV charging", "fleet electrification"

**Hour 7 — buffer + track:**
- Log every send in a simple spreadsheet: outlet, contact, date, template, response (Y/N), link (Y/N).
- Re-read each sent email for typos. (Do not "follow up" — that's spammy. If they want it, they reply.)

### What NOT to do

- Do not BCC or send to "info@" addresses on Tier 1 — goes to a black hole. Named reporter or skip.
- Do not attach a PDF / press release. No journalist reads them.
- Do not "circle back" — sending one good email beats sending three mediocre ones.
- Do not pitch the homepage. Pitch a specific page (data, calculator, or one grant guide).
- Do not pitch all 12 regional outlets. Two, max.
- Do not post the same link in multiple Reddit threads in one day. Account gets shadowbanned.
- Do not buy any "guest post" offers that will arrive in reply. Delete on sight.

### Honest response rates

- Trade press cold email with a real data hook: **8–15% reply rate**, of which maybe a third turn into a mention or link. So 6 trade sends → 0–2 links is the realistic distribution.
- Associations: **20–30% reply rate** (they're friendlier), but conversion to an actual public link is lower — often "we'll keep it on file." 0–1 links from 2 sends.
- Community: **0–2 links from organic participation**, plus referral traffic that doesn't show as a link.

**Expected total from one weekend: 1–4 followed dofollow/nofollow links, 5–15 referral visits in week 1.** Sometimes zero. That is normal.

## 4. The data-page seeding play (recurring news source)

`/data/uk-ev-installer-landscape/` refreshes weekly. Most weeks: nothing newsworthy. Occasionally: a real story. The job is to **notice**, not to manufacture.

### What counts as "meaningful change" (trigger thresholds)

Define these upfront so you don't fool yourself later:
- A region's installer count jumps or drops by **>5% week-on-week**, OR
- A previously zero-installer town gains its first installer, OR
- The **gap** between best- and worst-served region widens or narrows by >10%, OR
- A nationally-known installer (look for them in news) is added or removed.

Each weekly refresh: open `data/refresh_report.md`. 30 seconds. If none of the above triggered, do nothing — close the tab.

### When a trigger fires — the playbook

1. **Write one paragraph** of plain English describing the change. Add it as a dated entry at the top of the data page (a "Recent changes" log). This gives the URL a fresh reason to be re-crawled and gives journalists something quotable.
2. **One LinkedIn post**, factual: "OZEV added 14 commercial installers this week, 9 of them in [REGION]. That region is still [stat]. Source: GOV.UK list." Link to data page.
3. **One email** — only if the change is genuinely interesting — to the *single* trade-press reporter most likely to care. Reuse Template A with the new stat as the subject. Do not re-pitch reporters more than once per quarter.
4. **Tweet/Bluesky** the chart image with the stat. Tag no one.

Time cost per trigger: ~20 min. Frequency: realistically 4–8 triggers/year.

### What not to do

- Don't post every weekly refresh. The signal dies.
- Don't manufacture significance. If the change is small, say nothing.
- Don't email the same journalist twice within 8 weeks.

## 5. Blunt assessment

### Realistic outcome of one disciplined weekend

- **Links: 1–4.** Most likely 2. Zero is possible and not a failure signal on its own.
- **Shares (LinkedIn/X/Bluesky): 5–20** if the regional-inequality stat lands; <5 if it doesn't.
- **Referral traffic in week 1: 10–40 visits.**
- **Domain Rating / Authority change at 8 weeks: probably none yet** unless a Tier 2 site links.

### Search Console KPIs to watch

**Week 4 after outreach (≈ 8–10 weeks after deploy):**
- Impressions trending up week-on-week (any slope > 0 is fine this early)
- Indexed pages: should be >80% of the 1,152 submitted; if <50%, fix indexing before more outreach
- Average position for branded terms ("commercial ev installers UK") < 30
- At least 1 page in top 50 for a long-tail town query (e.g. "EV charger installer Stoke-on-Trent commercial")

**Week 8 after outreach:**
- Impressions ≥ 2x week-4 level
- At least 5 pages getting >0 clicks
- Average position for a regional query (e.g. "commercial EV installers North East") < 50
- Referring domains in GSC Links report: ≥ 3 (excluding pages.dev internals)

### "This didn't work — pivot" signals

Pivot when at **12 weeks post-outreach** you see *all* of:
- Zero referring domains gained
- Impressions flat or declining for 4 consecutive weeks
- No indexed town pages ranking in top 100 for "[town] commercial ev installer"
- No replies — not even rejections — to any of the 10 emails

If all four are true: the niche is not searched enough, or the site needs a different hook. Memory already documents the agricultural-solar fallback — that's the pivot. Don't pivot on one of these signals; require all four.

### One-line summary

One weekend of disciplined, honest outreach to ~10 named UK trade-press contacts with a real regional-inequality dataset typically yields 1–4 links and provides enough signal to know at 12 weeks whether to continue, double down, or pivot. Anything promising more is selling something.
