**Subject:** Only 5.2% of UK OZEV commercial EV installers are commercial-only — the rest are residential firms taking commercial work

Hi [first name],

I've put together a May 2026 snapshot of the UK OZEV-authorised commercial EV charger installer register and one finding stood out for the net-zero-business beat.

Of the 1,132 firms on the commercial register, only 59 — 5.2% — are commercial-only. The other 94.8% are residential-first installers that also accept commercial work. For a procurement team building a fleet electrification plan against the March 2027 grant deadlines, the addressable "commercial installer" pool is a lot smaller than the headline number implies, and it's distributed very unevenly: the South East holds 17.9% of the total, the North East 3.3%.
